# PM&R Oral Board Case Scoping Analysis

## Official ABPMR Part II Oral Exam Categories

Based on research from ABPMR, Springer Publishing, and AOBPMR:

1. Brain Impairments / CNS Disorders (includes TBI, stroke)
2. Spinal Cord Injury
3. Musculoskeletal Impairments / Sports Medicine
4. Neuromuscular Impairments / Electrodiagnosis
5. Pain / Spinal Impairments
6. Pediatric Rehabilitation
7. Amputee Care / Prosthetics
8. Cancer / Medically Complex Rehabilitation
9. Cardiovascular / Pulmonary Rehabilitation
10. Geriatric Rehabilitation
11. Soft Tissue Impairments
12. Durable Medical Equipment
