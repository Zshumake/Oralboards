### cardiopulm_transplant.dart

A patient on LVAD bridge-to-transplant would typically be **Status 4** under the current system (or the old Status 1B). **Correction**: Update to reflect the current UNOS 6-tier system -- "Status 4" is most appropriate for an LVAD-supported patient stable at home, or "Status 3" if hospitalized on LVAD with complications.

### burn_major_rehab.dart
**ISSUES FOUND**
1. **Curreri formula application**: The case states "Curreri formula: 25 kcal/kg/day + 40 kcal/%TBSA/day = ~3450 kcal/day." For a 74 kg patient with 40% TBSA: 25(74) + 40(40) = 1850 + 1600 = 3450. The math is correct. However, the Curreri formula is now considered to **overestimate** caloric needs and has largely been replaced by indirect calorimetry or the Toronto formula in modern burn care. While the formula itself is mathematically correct, a note that indirect calorimetry is preferred when available would be more current. **Minor issue -- not an error per se, but could be flagged as slightly outdated.**
